Food that owls eat

  • owls hunt mices since they are strictly carnivorous and need the vital fats, proteins, and taurine that small rodents provide to survive. Mice are nocturnal, making them active exactly when owls are, and they are perfectly adapted in size to be swallowed whole
  • Moths because they are highly efficient, nutritious, and active when owls are. For smaller owl species—like the Flammulated Owl or screech owls—moths are a primary food source. Moths are abundant, easy to catch on the wing, and provide an excellent source of protein for both adults and their nestlings.
  • Frogs because Owls hunt frogs primarily because they are opportunistic carnivores. Frogs are a highly nutritious, easily digestible source of protein and fat. Additionally, since frogs are primarily active at night and highly vocal, they become effortless, acoustic targets for an owl's acute hearing.
